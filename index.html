Sciar Tips

For restaurant owners

Card tips shared with your team every week, with every share explained.

You set the split rule. Sciar Tips works out each person's share from the card tips on your terminal, and each member of staff sees their own payout and how it was calculated, on their phone.

Talk to us: hello@sciar.example See the staff view (demo)
  • Every card tip went into the staff pool.In our 12-week pilot, €31,754.13 of card tips came in and €31,754.09 was paid out to staff. The 4 cents is rounding to the cent.1
  • Every person, every week, for 12 weeks.In the pilot, a tip payout was worked out for every member of staff in each of the 12 weeks from 14 August to 5 November 2028.2
  • Staff can see how their share is worked out, and raise a concern.Our alpha staff view shows each person their own points, hours and payout, and lets them raise a concern from their phone.3

Where we are: Sciar Tips is an alpha. It has been piloted with one restaurant in Dublin 7. In the alpha, the concern feature is simulated: it saves on the person's own phone and sends nothing.
